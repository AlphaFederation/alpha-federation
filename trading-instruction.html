Guidelines & Execution

Comprehensive Trading Instructions & Notes

Extended operational rules, risk boundaries, psychological checklists, and multi-timeframe methodologies for absolute market discipline.

01. Risk Management & Capital Preservation

Never enter any trade without defining your risk parameters beforehand. Capital preservation is the absolute foundation for long-term market survival and compounding consistency.

  • Position Sizing Calculations: Always compute position size precisely based on account equity and stop distance.
  • Risk Cap Threshold: Never risk more than 1% to 2% of total active capital on any individual trade setup.
  • Immediate Stop-Loss Execution: Place stop-losses physically on the exchange immediately upon order execution.

Detailed Note: Ignoring stop-losses during high-impact news releases or flash crashes leads directly to devastating account drawdowns. Treat your capital like a business inventory.

02. Market Analysis, Confluence & Timing

Patience is paramount. Wait exclusively for high-probability setups aligning with your macro bias rather than trading out of boredom or FOMO.

  • Multi-Timeframe Structure: Map higher timeframes (Daily/4H) first before looking for triggers on lower charts (15m/5m).
  • Liquidity & Support Zones: Target entries near institutional order blocks, liquidity pools, and proven historical support/resistance levels.
  • News Blackout Windows: Avoid opening fresh intraday positions 15 minutes before or after high-impact economic data releases.

Detailed Note: Missing a move is infinitely superior to taking a forced loss. Protect mental energy by staying flat during choppy consolidation ranges.

03. Psychological Discipline & Execution

Execution must be strictly mechanical, detached from emotional highs and lows. Adhere rigorously to your pre-market and post-market routines.

  • Comprehensive Trade Journaling: Document every entry, exit price, psychological state, setup grade, and screenshot.
  • Accepting Losing Outcomes: Treat losses as a normal, budgeted cost of operating a trading business.
  • Hard Daily Stop Limits: Step away from the screens immediately upon hitting your maximum daily loss threshold.

Detailed Note: Revenge trading after a loss is the primary catalyst for total account liquidation. Respect your daily circuit breakers and step away.

04. Trade Management & Profit Harvesting

Managing an open position requires as much precision and focus as the initial entry strategy.

  • Scaling Out: Take partial profits at predetermined risk-to-reward milestones (e.g., 1:2 or 1:3 targets).
  • Breakeven Stops: Move stop-loss to breakeven only after price cleanly breaks key structural resistance.
  • Trailing Stop Discipline: Use structural market swings rather than arbitrary time intervals to trail open profit.

Detailed Note: Letting a winning trade turn into a loser due to greed destroys long-term statistical expectancy. Secure partial profits systematically.

05. Portfolio Diversification & Asset Allocation

Avoid over-concentrating risk across heavily correlated assets or single market sectors.

  • Sector Spread: Distribute capital across non-correlated instruments or asset classes to mitigate systemic shock.
  • Leverage Restrictions: Restrict maximum portfolio leverage to safe, manageable bounds depending on market volatility.
  • Cash Reserve Buffer: Maintain a healthy cash allocation to capitalize on unexpected major market discounts.

Detailed Note: Over-leveraging correlated pairs multiplies hidden exposure and can wipe out accounts simultaneously during black swan events.
