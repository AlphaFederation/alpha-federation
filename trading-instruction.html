Trading Instructions

Follow these core principles and structured guidelines for market participation.

01. Risk Management First

Never enter any trade without defining your risk parameters beforehand. Capital preservation is the absolute priority for long-term survival and consistency in the markets.

  • Always calculate position size relative to your total trading capital.
  • Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your account on a single setup.
  • Always use predefined stop-loss orders to eliminate emotional decision-making.

02. Market Analysis & Setup

Patience is key. Wait for high-probability setups that align with your overall market bias rather than forcing trades out of boredom or FOMO.

  • Perform multi-timeframe analysis before executing any position.
  • Identify key support, resistance, and structural trend zones.
  • Avoid trading during high-impact news events unless managing existing intraday risk.

03. Discipline & Execution

Execution must be mechanical and detached from emotional highs and lows. Stick to your trading checklist without deviation.

  • Log every trade with entry, exit, reasoning, and psychological state.
  • Accept losses as a normal cost of doing business in the financial markets.
  • Step away from the screens after hitting daily loss limits or profit goals.
