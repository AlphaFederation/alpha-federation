Trading Instruction & Guidelines

Step-by-step instructions to help you navigate financial markets securely and effectively.

1. Understanding Market Basics

Before executing any trade, ensure you understand asset classes, order types (Market, Limit, Stop-Loss), and risk management fundamentals.

2. Setting Up Your Trading Account

Make sure your brokerage account (such as Upstox or other supported platforms) is fully verified with KYC documents completed and active for segment trading.

3. Risk Management Rules

Never risk more capital than you can afford to lose on a single trade. Always utilize stop-loss mechanisms to protect your portfolio against sudden market volatility.
